What am I looking at?

Each control tick sends a batch of tokens from the car to a mixture-of-experts model. Only a few experts fit in memory; loading an absent expert adds latency. If the total exceeds the control deadline, the car hesitates.

LRU evicts the least recently used expert. RT-MoE protects hot experts and uses free slots for prefetch.

Inspired by Autoware.Flex (RTCSA 2025) and RT-MoE (RTSS 2026). This is an illustrative simulation, not a benchmark.
