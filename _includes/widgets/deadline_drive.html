What am I looking at?

Each control tick sends a batch of tokens from the locomotive to a mixture-of-experts engine. Only a few experts fit in the engine; loading an absent expert adds latency. If the total exceeds the control deadline, the express hesitates.

LRU evicts the least recently used expert. RT-MoE protects hot experts and uses free slots for prefetch.

The line outside the cab is also the research route: UESTC (the DNN years), CityU Shenzhen × Hon Hai (the Autoware / ATER project years), and CityU MLab (the real-time MoE years). The locomotive stops briefly at each station and checkpoint.

Inspired by Autoware.Flex (RTCSA 2025) and RT-MoE (RTSS 2026). This is an illustrative simulation, not a benchmark.
