the paper that started it all

Semi-Federated Scheduling of Parallel Real-Time Tasks on Multiprocessors

Xu Jiang · Nan Guan · Xiang Long · Wang Yi

IEEE Real-Time Systems Symposium (RTSS) 2017 · DOI 10.1109/RTSS.2017.00015

In my first year as a master's student, I came across this paper by Nan Guan and his co-authors. I still remember how elegant the idea felt: a heavy DAG task with processing capacity x + δ is granted only x dedicated processors, while the remaining δ part is scheduled together with light tasks on shared processors. It is still one of my favourite papers in real-time scheduling — and it is the reason I decided to do research and, eventually, to pursue a Ph.D. with Nan at CityU. Years later I never became a pure real-time scheduling theorist, but I am still working every day toward becoming a real-time systems researcher. This paper is still the standard I measure my own work against.
